    Hello!

    Welcome to Pixel Swarm!

    • Pick a theme in Levels: Flowers, Fruits, Animals, Sweets or Ocean.
    • Every theme starts on Easy. Finish it to unlock Medium, then Hard.
    • Let's learn how to play with your first level!

    How to Play

    • Goal: clear every pixel of the picture to win.
    • Play: tap a block at the front of a line. It goes to a free slot and bees fly out of the tree to eat pixels of its color. The number is how many pixels it eats.
    • From the bottom up: bugs enter the picture from the bottom, so a pixel can only be eaten once the pixels in the way are cleared. Choose your colors in the right order!
    • You lose when all slots are full and none of their colors can be reached: you're Out of Slots.
    • Puzzles: Medium and Hard levels add surprises like mystery and frozen blocks, linked blocks, pipes, keys and locks, wood blocks, cookies, big and hidden pixels. A New Feature card explains each one the first time you meet it.
    • Boosters (they unlock as you play): Add Slot gives an extra slot, Shuffle mixes the colors in the line, Wind blows away every pixel of one color, Free Pick lets you take any block in the line. Stuck? Open an AD slot.
